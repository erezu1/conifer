Tab bars for Conifer

Tap a tab, or drag sideways on the text below it: the mark follows your finger and settles on the nearest tab, as the reader does. The same fonts, colours and line weight as the reader.

Now: the pill

A grey track with a raised white button: the heaviest thing on the screen.

A. The underline

Three labels and one fine deep-blue line under the one you read, gliding between them. The search row's underline, in the same family.

B. The depth line

The icon's trunk laid on its side: a node per tab, the line lit up to how deep you read. Gist is one node; Paper the whole line.

C. The depth line, quieter

B with small caps labels and filled dots only, the current label in blue: the least weight that still shows depth.
