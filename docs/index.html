Papers grown into trees

Every paper here is a tree you can read at any depth. The trunk is the gist: the context, the result, why it matters. The branches are a one-page summary whose sentences show the part of the paper they stand for. The foliage is the paper itself, and the roots are its appendices and proofs, folded until you want them. Definitions, background notes and anticipated questions hang off the text, and a dial sets how much is shown.
