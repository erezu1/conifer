Project Conifer

From the gist down

Each paper opens on a short card and goes deeper when you want it to. Switch between the depths with the tabs at the top of a paper.

  • Gistthe question, what the paper finds and why it matters: a minute.
  • Summaryone page; tap a sentence to see the paragraphs it stands for.
  • Paperthe full text, with a short summary under each section, definitions on dotted terms, background notes and questions.

The text of each paper is its authors'. The gists, summaries, definitions, notes and questions are drafted by AI, marked as such, and not yet reviewed by the authors.

    The eigenvalues of a large random Hermitian matrix are not scattered at random: their density has a universal shape, found by Wigner in the 1950s for nuclear spectra and met again today wherever a Hamiltonian is modelled by a random matrix.
