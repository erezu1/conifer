# Possible slips found while drafting the trees

Each tree's drafter recorded, in `FLAGS` in its layers.py, the places where the paper seemed inconsistent or where it was unsure. They are questions for the authors, not corrections; nothing in the papers was changed.

## arXiv:2609.29859: The cosmological necklace problem

- `sect1.1unentropicsols-p4` The introduction gives each $k$-cover entropy $k|I_0|$, with $I_0$ the Euclidean wormhole action, which (2.19) makes $-\pi(1-q)/2G$; but the saddles the contour selects have $-I=k\pi/2G$ for every $q$ ((2.36), (2.37)), the difference being the Lorentzian detour's $\pi q/2G$. walls-p1 and sect3.3kineticnum then write $|I_0|$ for the $q$-independent value. Is $I_0$ meant to include the detour? The layers give (2.19) for the Euclidean necklaces and $\pi/2G$ for the dominant saddle.
- `sect-1.intro-p2` “their entropy is greater than the Gibbons-Hawking entropy”: with (2.19) a $k$-necklace has $k\pi(1-q)/2G$, which exceeds $\pi/2G$ only for $k(1-q)>1$, so not every $k>1$ necklace at large $q$. Is the comparison meant with the $q$-independent saddle actions, or for large enough $k$? The layers say only that the entropy grows without bound.
- `sect-3.3-p2` (2.80) is labelled $GI_{a\to-a}(2\pi)$, but the Gibbons–Hawking saddle studied there is at $N=\pi$; and the text says the second line “is exact and gives $\pi/2$” while the result is $-\pi/2$ (the second-line integrand also looks positive). Presumably $I_{a\to-a}(\pi)$ and $-\pi/2$.
- `app-contours-p5` “choosing the contour $N=-\varepsilon+i\mathbb R$, the leading contribution is $k=-1$”: within the bouncing ($a\to-a$) sector; overall the standard sector's $k=0$, with $I=0$, dominates, as conclusion-p2 and sect5-concl-p2 say. Worth saying “in this sector”.
- `sect4-magnetic-p2` The abstract speaks of necklaces “sourced by … Yang-Mills instantons”, while section 4 builds a purely magnetic configuration, the gauge field equal to the spin connection (the meron in $d=4$, footnote 22), and calls them magnetic wormholes. The layers use “Yang–Mills fields” and “magnetic necklaces”; is “instantons” intended?
- `fig-app2` Caption: “$a_0\to a_\text{mas}$”, presumably $a_\text{max}$.
- `appa1-p1` In 5d proper-time gauge (A.13) has saddles at $N=k\pi$, including $N=\pi$ with the same action as the $a\to-a$ saddle (A.17) at $N=\pi$; so here the single sphere seems to appear in both sectors, unlike in 3d (standard-solutions-p1, section 2.3: “the single Euclidean sphere does not arise in this $a\to a$ sector”). Harmless for the conclusion, since app-contours-p2 shows $N=\pm\varepsilon+i\mathbb R$ picks only $k\le0$ there, but the drafter was unsure the parity rule is meant to hold in this gauge.
